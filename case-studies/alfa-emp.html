Alfa-Bank EMP: hiring, fully online

EMP is Alfa-Bank’s system for hiring remote sales agents and other external staff — from the invitation email to signing employment documents with an electronic signature. I designed the candidate questionnaire for phones and the desktop cabinets for managers, functional managers and agents.

Company
Alfa-Bank, Alfa Digital
Role
Product Designer
Platform

Years

Overview

Hiring without an office or a paper trail

Alfa-Bank’s partners hire hundreds of sales agents across the country. That used to mean office visits, piles of scans sent by email and manual checks. The goal of the project was to automate hiring remote workers through the EMP employment system.

The candidate gets a temporary link, signs in with an SMS code, photographs their passport, fills in their SNILS number and address, picks a salary account, signs the consents and issues an electronic signature in Sign.me. Check results come back as notifications.

Value for the bank

What EMP delivers
  • Multiple growth of the agent network
  • Shorter hiring time
  • Fewer documents
  • A managed, systematic process
  • Lower operational load on the business
  • Lower load on HR

Process

Product design inside Scrum

I worked in a Scrum team with analysts, developers and the business. Design ran one step ahead of the sprint: research and hypotheses went into the backlog, schemes and mockups into planning, design reviews and tests into the release, followed by support and documentation.

Design improvements

Logo, footer socials, colours, switching between partners

New scenarios

Password reset, time tracking, offboarding, agent cabinet

Handoff

Jira tasks, component specs, front-end fixes

Testing

Flows with metrics, drop-off points and user quotes

Mobile

Candidate questionnaire: five steps on a phone

The candidate always sees which step they’re on and what comes next. Each step is one task, with help and an exit at the bottom of every screen.

States and errors

We designed everything that goes off-plan: empty fields, an invalid SNILS, an unreadable photo, a failed upload, and a persistent notice that explains what to do next.

Desktop

Cabinets for the people who hire

Managers, functional managers and agents share one frame: dark navigation on the left, search and filters at the top, tables with statuses, and notifications. Roles switch easily, and new sections slot in without rework.

Functional manager

Business line

At the top — the business line’s key parameters: headcount, line code, contract length, agent employment rate and the security check code. Below — the line’s partners, searchable by name, tax ID or contract number.

Functional manager · Manager

New request

A request takes a minute: passport, email and phone. The data is checked first — only then does “Create” become active. Several candidates can be added at once.

Functional manager · Manager

Agent requests

All requests in one table: search by name, phone, email, city or number, filters, status and history. A request can be declined right from its row.

Manager

Hired agents

The staff list with city, contacts and contract end date. A tooltip on the status shows when the agent was hired.

All roles

Notification centre

New request lists, phone change requests, documents ready to sign and processing stops — grouped by day, with business line and time. Urgent items stand out with colour and an icon.

Dashboard

A dashboard built from user stories

Before drawing any charts, we collected scenarios from functional managers and the product owner: every block on the dashboard has to answer a specific question.

Find an agent by surname to see which partner they belong to.
Export last month’s requests to calculate the cost of e-signature licences.
Compare how many agents each partner hired to see who is biggest.
See how many requests the security check declined, out of all completed ones.
See who else has access to my business line.
Get analytics across all lines to plan next year’s licences.
Communications

Emails that guide the process

Candidates spend most of the process outside the system, so emails became part of the interface. I designed more than twenty templates: invitations for agents and managers, logins, rejections for different reasons, signing reminders, and service notices for managers and support.

  • The invitation lists what you’ll need and the candidate requirements.
  • One main button and clear next steps.
  • One template and layout for every role.
