Domina Invest: a partner platform for selling hotel real estate

I designed a whole product ecosystem from scratch: the real estate broker workspace, the operator admin panel, the website and project landings, social media, an onboarding video and agency decks — all in one visual language built on the Domina brand.

Company
Domina Invest · Domina Group

Year

Brief

Sell a hotel room through a real estate broker who has never sold hotels

Domina Group has managed hotels for 35 years and launched Domina Invest: a private investor buys a room in a hotel, and the management company rents it out and pays a contractual income. The main sales channel is real estate brokers and agencies.

The problem: real estate brokers know apartments, but can't explain hotel unit economics, don't trust a new counterparty and have no single place for live lots, availability and sales materials. The task was a platform that gets a real estate broker selling hotel real estate in 20 minutes — plus the marketing that brings real estate brokers in.

  • 35years of hotel management
  • ~7%average real estate broker commission
  • ~17%investor yield per year
  • 1.4M ₽+real estate broker reward per deal

Research

How real estate brokers already work with new-build platforms

I broke down the real estate broker workspaces of YGROUP and TrendAgent across five scenarios: sign-up and navigation, video onboarding, lot and chessboard, showcase and filters, client fixation. Separately, I built a moodboard of premium hospitality — the product had to feel like a 5-star hotel, not a CRM.

What I took into the product

  • Real estate brokers need a passwordless sign-in and instant access to at least a demo catalogue — otherwise they leave before seeing a single object.
  • Commission and yield are the primary filters, not secondary fields on a card.
  • The chessboard and the client selection are daily working tools.
  • No competitor explains the product: nobody teaches real estate brokers to sell a new asset class. That became our niche.

Real estate broker personas

Commercial broker

Leases offices and warehouses. A client needs to park 40M ₽ with passive income — apartments don't fit.

Premium broker

Closes 100M ₽+ deals twice a year, while clients with up to 50M ₽ keep coming and get nothing to buy.

Resale realtor

Average fee 150–200K ₽. Knows apartments, but can't answer a question about apart-hotels.

Investor personas

Tired landlord

Owns 3–5 flats and is tired of repairs and tenant calls.

Diversifier

Has flats, leased retail and commercial — looking for a new instrument within real estate.

Capital protector

Wants returns above a bank deposit with regular cash flow.

Status buyer

Values the prestige of owning a 5-star hotel room and capital protection.

Hypotheses

Six hypotheses the product is built on

H1

Real estate brokers don't sell hotels because they lack expertise, not clients

Solution: the real estate broker fixes a client in one click, and a Domina expert runs the presentation and closes the deal with them. It became the core offer of the landing and decks.

Metric: share of real estate brokers making a first fixation

H2

A short sign-up with demo access beats a full questionnaire

Solution: 3 steps (email/phone, role, details), Yandex ID sign-in and a “New (Demo)” status with flagship hotels. Full access unlocks after a qualification call from the admin panel.

Metric: sign-up → qualified conversion

H3

A video tour of the workspace shortens time to first fixation

Solution: a 4-minute onboarding video with a presenter and UI highlights, a pop-up on the first visit to the showcase, and onboarding steps in the profile.

Metric: time from sign-up to first fixation

H4

Real estate brokers need to show objects to clients without revealing commission

Solution: a “demo mode” that hides rewards, and shareable selections where the real estate broker sees the client's views and picks.

Metric: selections opened by clients

H5

A commission equal to the developer's removes the trust barrier

Solution: a “Domina Invest commission equals the developer's” note on the object page, transparent fixation statuses and a 24-hour payout promise.

Metric: drop-off at the fixation step

H6

Hospitality rewards retain real estate brokers better than one-off bonuses

Solution: 0.1% bonuses per deal redeemable for nights at Domina hotels, a seasonal leaderboard with a top-100 trip, and a 0.3% referral programme.

Metric: repeat deals per real estate broker

Real estate broker platform

A workspace that feels like a hotel and works like a CRM

01

Sign-in & sign-up

Three steps with a progress bar, role choice (real estate broker or agency) and Yandex ID. The optional “website or map listing” field speeds up agency verification.

02

Object showcase

Filters that matter to real estate brokers: investor yield, commission, stage, star rating. Each card shows lot price, reward and yield up front, and “Exclusive” and “Higher commission” tags hint at what to sell first.

03

Object page

The numbers a real estate broker needs on a call — buy-back after 10 years, reward, yield, management company — sit in a dedicated strip. Below: gallery, specs, amenity tags and the entry to the chessboard.

04

Room chessboard

A floor-by-floor grid with price and area in every cell, filters by category, area, floor and price, and a grid/list toggle. Data comes from Profitbase. I designed light, dark and mobile versions.

05

Client selections

The real estate broker bundles lots into a selection and shares a link. The list shows link status, views and what the client picked — “Marked Bashni Yes today at 12:40”. Every selection becomes a reason for the next call.

06

Client fixation

One form instead of every developer's own rules: objects, budget, payment method and presentation format — online or in the hotel lobby. After submission the client moves through 9 statuses, from uniqueness check to commission payout.

07

Loyalty programme

Bonus balance, a reward ladder from a night at Domina St. Petersburg to a trip to Sharm El Sheikh, accrual history, partner leaderboard and a referral link. On mobile, developer promos live in a carousel.

Iterations

Reviewing the built product: As Is → To Be

After the first build I ran a design review on production. “My clients” showed empty metrics and didn't help manage clients: no statuses, no fixation deadlines, no notes. To Be added meaningful metrics, developer promos, the full status model and an inline editable note.

Admin panel

An operator admin: 17 screens so the platform runs without a developer

Operators qualify new partners, add objects through a 5-tab form with a readiness indicator, check the card “as a real estate broker”, toggle visibility and Sold Out, and configure the loyalty programme. Every change is logged with the operator's name.

Design system

From a hotel brand book to a product system

The Domina brand book was made for hotels and print: Bodoni, Futura and five brand colours. I translated it into a product system: Jost and Inter for UI, a 9-step gold scale, a neutral scale, status colours and components — from chessboard cells to object cards.

  • 70variables: primitives, semantic, spacing
  • 30text styles
  • 2themes: light and dark
Website

A real estate broker landing and project landings for investors

For real estate brokers: a landing with an earnings calculator, story-style testimonials (“Fixed a phone number and earned 7%”) and a 4-step process. For investors: 8 landings for hotels and complexes in St. Petersburg, Tomsk, Novosibirsk, Agoy, Yevpatoria, Crimea, Pushkino and the Well apart-hotel.

Social media

Stories and posts: Bodoni for emotion, numbers set large

I assembled guidelines (logo, colours, type, pattern, pictograms) and three series: the Domina Invest image series, project stories for Severny Port and Mira, and an Instagram grid with useful content for real estate brokers — e.g. “5 client objections and how to answer them”. Partner real estate brokers reuse these assets in their own channels.

Video

Onboarding video: script and a 19-frame storyboard

I wrote a ≈5-minute script and storyboard: each frame lists timing, voice-over, on-screen action and camera angle. The presenter is Domina Invest's CEO, and UI elements are highlighted as they're mentioned. The video opens as a pop-up on the first visit to the showcase and serves hypothesis H3.

Sales decks

Agency decks: selling a new asset class in 20 slides

Three personalised versions for specific agency heads. The story: why “buy a flat to rent” no longer works → the hotel real-estate market is growing → why Domina → how the platform works → rewards and the referral network.
